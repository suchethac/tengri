# Lyu 2018 and Haro 11 templates; Rieke 2009 normalSFG templates

These arrays are converted from the public [`AGN_templates` repository](https://github.com/karlan/AGN_templates), retrieved on 2026-10-07. The reddened AGN files are under [`AGN_reddend_lib`](https://github.com/karlan/AGN_templates/tree/master/AGN_reddend_lib), the intrinsic comparison table is [`AGN_torus_faceon.temp.full.txt`](https://github.com/karlan/AGN_templates/blob/master/AGN_intrinsic/AGN_torus_faceon.temp.full.txt), and the Haro 11 source is [`Haro11.temp.full.txt`](https://github.com/karlan/AGN_templates/blob/master/host_galaxy/lometal_starburst/Haro11.temp.full.txt). The conversion script is [`scripts/build_lyu2018_templates.py`](../../../../scripts/build_lyu2018_templates.py).

`lyu2018_agn.h5` contains the NORMAL, WDD, and HDD reddened template families from `AGN_reddend_lib/`. All 123 source spectra were checked; each has 119 wavelength points and the three family indices each provide 41 tau values from 0 to 10 in steps of 0.25. For each family the HDF5 preserves the tau index, original relative F_nu values, and native wavelength axis converted from microns to Angstroms. It also stores `c0_reference`, the absolute native-frequency trapezoidal integral of the tau=0 row. That one family-specific value is used at every tau; it is not a per-template renormalization. The reddened source tables do not state an absolute flux unit. The public repository’s 5000-point intrinsic AGN table is labeled F_nu in Jy; the tau=0 reddened-to-intrinsic ratios are approximately 0.9340 (NORMAL), 0.1964 (HDD), and 0.4443 (WDD). This comparison supports treating the reddened files as relative F_nu; it is not an absolute calibration or a runtime input.

`haro11.h5` keeps the full 5000-row Haro 11 table under `source/`. Its root datasets contain only rows with wavelength greater than 5 microns, matching the source header’s stated validity range. The `wavelength_aa` array is converted from microns, while `flux_nu_relative` preserves the published Jansky-like values without an additional scale.

The AGN family source papers are Lyu, Rieke & Shi (2017), [doi:10.3847/1538-4357/835/2/257](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/835/2/257), and Lyu & Rieke (2018), [doi:10.3847/1538-4357/aae075](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aae075). The Haro 11 source paper is Lyu, Rieke & Alberts (2016), [doi:10.3847/0004-637X/816/2/85](https://doi.org/10.3847/0004-637X/816/2/85).

## Public normal star-forming-galaxy templates

`rieke2009.h5` packages the 14 public tables in [`host_galaxy/normal_SFGs`](https://github.com/karlan/AGN_templates/tree/master/host_galaxy/normal_SFGs), retrieved on 2026-10-08. The source files are `rieke-F09.75_temp.txt` through `rieke-F13.00_temp.txt` in 0.25-dex steps. Thirteen files contain 5000 rows; `rieke-F12.50_temp.txt` contains 3000 rows after its `FORPRINT:` header and starts at 1.0011519 μm. Source wavelength and second-column arrays are retained unchanged under `source/`. Runtime spectra are interpolated in wavelength from each native axis onto the first table's 5000-point axis. The public files identify the first column as wavelength and have no units for the second column; Tengri treats that column as a relative Fν shape and normalizes each blended spectrum on the model grid to the independently supplied total IR luminosity.

The six tables from log10(L_IR/L_sun)=9.75 through 11.00 each contain three finite negative values at 5.18203, 5.19398, and 5.20595 μm. They are localized shortwave subtraction residuals, not floating-point roundoff. The raw rows remain unchanged; the converter clips these 18 values to zero only in runtime spectra. No fixed shortwave cutoff is applied: the first positive source wavelength is 5.21795 μm in those six tables and 4.02254 μm in the remaining templates. The converter is [`scripts/build_rieke2009_templates.py`](../../../../scripts/build_rieke2009_templates.py).

Rieke et al. (2009) describe the luminosity-dependent SFG library ([doi:10.1088/0004-637X/692/1/556](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/692/1/556)). Lyu et al. (2022, Appendix A) describe a modified Rieke library with dust emission extended below 5 μm and stellar emission removed or reduced from the mid-IR ([arXiv:2209.06219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.06219)). Lyu et al. (2024, §3.1 and Table 1) use the normal-SFG template for their primary sample, with `L_IR_temp` fixed at 11.25 and a separate free SFG normalization ([ApJ 966, 229](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad3643)). Tengri exposes the shape selector `dust_log_L_ir_template` separately from the total IR amplitude. The public text files do not establish that their shortwave spectra are identical to the later Lyu 2024 implementation.
